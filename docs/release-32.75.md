# NikoTube 32.75

Stable Android package: `org.smarttube.stable`. Requires Android 8.0 or newer.

- Swipe down at least 96dp in fullscreen to exit to portrait, with a 200ms fade and downward transition.
- The gesture does not run in picture-in-picture mode.

The stable signing identity is retained. Beta installations are unchanged.

On a Galaxy A52, a downward swipe exited playback fullscreen and restored the system bars and watch details. Like/subscribe account-side changes were not confirmed after sign-in.
